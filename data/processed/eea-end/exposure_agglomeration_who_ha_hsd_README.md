## Gaps / method choices

1. **Industry** — no WHO ENG 2018 %HA/%HSD ERF for industry; skipped (6090 input rows ignored).
2. **Age** — no ≥18 structure in END exposure table; counts use **total** END band people (`age_filter=none_total_end_exposed`). EEA BoD often targets adults ≥18.
3. **Band floors** — voluntary bands from Lden 45 and Lnight 40 used when populated; not truncated to WHO guideline values (road 53/45 etc.). Midpoints: 47…77 (Lden), 42…72 (Lnight); open ends `>75`→77, `>70`→72.
4. **ERFs** — Guski et al. 2017 (%HA) and Basner & McGuire 2018 (%HSD) polynomials as used in EEA HA/HSD practice; % clamped to [0, 100].
